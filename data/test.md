## ✨ New Features
### 🔌 Power Device Library v4.00.00
- **raC_Dvc_PF755T_CM**: PowerFlex® 755T CIP Motion Drive  
- **raC_Dvc_1834_AENTR**: Stratix® 4400 EtherNet/IP In-Cabinet Gateway  
- **raC_Dvc_10xE_INT**: 100E-INT-D & 104E-INT-D EtherNet/IP In-Cabinet Contactors  
- **raC_Dvc_800F Series**:  
  - 2-position selector  
  - 3-position selector  
  - Multi-Operator 2-Function Button  
  - Pilot Light  
  - Pushbutton  

### 📊 PM5000 PQEM
- Advanced faceplates for **FactoryTalk Optix**  

### 🛠️ Other Enhancements
- Active/Passive Power Devices with **Studio 5000 ACM**  
- **CompactLogix 5380/5480 Dual-IP Mode Support**  
- Standardization improvements  
- Revised and consolidated videos
